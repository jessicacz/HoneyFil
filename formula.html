Why amounts matter

Presence is not the whole formula

A formula is defined by its complete ingredient list, serving size and amounts. Two products can mention similar ingredients while using different amounts or forms. That is why a current label is more useful than an ingredient list copied from an older page.

Verification checklist

  • Match the product name and version.
  • Read the full Supplement Facts or ingredient panel.
  • Record amounts and serving size.
  • Check directions and warnings.
  • Confirm the information is current.
